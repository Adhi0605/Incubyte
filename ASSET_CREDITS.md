# Representative car photograph credits

The catalog contains synthetic Indian new/used listings. Every photograph is representative model imagery: it is not a photograph of an individual listed vehicle. Generation, trim, colour and market may differ. No manufacturer or photographer endorsement is implied.

Photos were obtained from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-25 using its imageinfo API. Local files are the Commons-generated thumbnails (requested width 960 px); no local image edits were made. Each source page identifies its author and selected reusable license. Where a source offered multiple licenses, the Creative Commons license below was selected. CC BY-SA photographs and their thumbnails retain that license. Attribution and license links also travel in each listing's gallery metadata.

`frontend/public/cars/sources.json` retains original/download URLs, author, source metadata and SHA-256 hashes for verification. Model information links point to the relevant manufacturer (Tata uses its corporate homepage because its model microsites failed TLS verification during sourcing); these photos were not copied from manufacturer marketing sites.
